Pattern/Like Privacy

Last updated: September 4, 2026.

Pattern/Like encrypts submitted birth details and selected-place results under an account-specific key. Account deletion removes that Pattern/Like data, subject to the deletion workflow and short-lived operational proof described in the product.

Optional Geoapify birthplace search

If you enable it, Pattern/Like sends the city or place text you type and your language preference, when available, to Geoapify. After selection, it sends the selected Geoapify place identifier and language preference to retrieve the place. It does not send your birth date or time, device location, Pattern/Like identifiers, consent identifier, or app-owned search-session token. A provider place identifier can itself encode location information.

Geoapify receives the request content, Pattern/Like's API credential, and network metadata such as the server IP. Its privacy policy describes API request logging for access control, usage counting, troubleshooting, and performance, with successful request logs generally kept no longer than 24 hours. That is not a guarantee for all records. Geoapify describes EU hosting and use of infrastructure providers including Cloudflare; Pattern/Like does not promise that every network hop remains in one region.

Pattern/Like does not store your query or unselected suggestions. It stores the selected place, coordinates, confidence, and qualifiers encrypted for up to 24 hours as an onboarding handoff, with durable selected values copied into the encrypted birth profile. Deleting your Pattern/Like account does not delete Geoapify's separate records. Prior Google permissions do not authorize Geoapify search.

You may decline or revoke this permission and continue with manual place, coordinate, and time-zone entry.

Pattern/Like Privacy

Last updated: August 28, 2026.

Pattern/Like encrypts submitted birth details and selected-place results under an account-specific key. Account deletion removes that Pattern/Like data, subject to the deletion workflow and short-lived operational proof described in the product.

Optional Google birthplace search

If you enable it, Pattern/Like sends the city or place text you type and your language preference, when available, to Google Places Autocomplete. After selection, it sends only Google's opaque Place ID to Google Geocoding. It does not send your birth date or time, coordinates, device location, Pattern/Like identifiers, consent identifier, or app-owned search-session token.

Google acts as an independent controller for information it receives, including request content, the Pattern/Like project credential, and network metadata such as the Worker IP. Google's separate retention and use are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and applicable Google terms. The reviewed service does not provide a configurable Maps data-residency control; processing may involve cross-border transfers.

Pattern/Like does not store your query or unselected suggestions. It stores the selected formatted address, coordinates, confidence, and qualifiers encrypted for up to 24 hours as an onboarding handoff, with the durable selected values copied into the encrypted birth profile. Deleting your Pattern/Like account does not delete records independently controlled by Google.

You may decline or revoke this permission and continue with manual place, coordinate, and time-zone entry.
